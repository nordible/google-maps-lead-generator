# 📱 Viral Reel Scripts for Lead Gen App

## Reel 1: The "Profit & Savings" Hook (Best for Business Owners)
*   **Visual:** Opening shot of a expensive SaaS pricing page (like Apollo or Clay showing $149/mo).
*   **Audio:** "Stop throwing ₹12,000 away every single month."
*   **Text Overlay:** "Stop paying for leads ❌"
*   **Visual:** Switch to your app running in Docker or the UI.
*   **Audio:** "I built a custom AI scraper that finds owner emails, social media, and phone numbers directly from Google Maps for 1/10th the cost."
*   **Visual:** Show the Excel sheet filling up automatically.
*   **Audio:** "It doesn't just scrape; it visits the website and uses AI to find the contacts you actually need."
*   **CTA:** "Comment 'LEADS' below and I’ll DM you the setup guide for free."

## Reel 2: The "Hidden Contact" Hook (Best for Sales Teams)
*   **Visual:** Someone manually clicking through Google Maps looking frustrated.
*   **Audio:** "Google Maps only gives you the business number. But what if you want the owner's personal email?"
*   **Text Overlay:** "Find HIDDEN contacts 🕵️‍♂️"
*   **Visual:** Speed ramp of the app extracting an email that wasn't on the Maps listing.
*   **Audio:** "My AI-powered lead generator visits every website, finds the social links, and extracts the direct emails automatically."
*   **Visual:** Show the final exported file with 100+ rows.
*   **CTA:** "I'm giving away the full setup guide. Just comment 'GROW' and it's yours."

## Reel 3: The "Lazy Setup" Hook (Best for Non-Techies)
*   **Visual:** A terminal window running one command: `docker-compose up`.
*   **Audio:** "One command. That’s all it takes to build your own lead generation machine."
*   **Text Overlay:** "Setup in 60 seconds ⚡"
*   **Visual:** The Streamlit UI popping up and a search for "Plumbers in Dubai" starting.
*   **Audio:** "No coding required. Just plug in your keys and let the AI work while you sleep."
*   **CTA:** "Want the guide and the one-click installer? Comment 'READY' and check your DMs."

---

## 💡 Pro-Tips for Reels:
1.  **Fast Pacing:** Every 2-3 seconds, change the visual.
2.  **Sound:** Use trending "Business" or "Tech" audio.
3.  **Captions:** Always use big, colorful captions on screen.
