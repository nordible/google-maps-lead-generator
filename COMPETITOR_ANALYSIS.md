## **Feature Comparison**

### **1. Apollo.io**
*   **Pros:** Massive database, CRM integration, built-in email sending.
*   **Cons:** High monthly recurring cost. Limits how many emails you can export per month.
*   **VS This Tool:** Apollo is better for general B2B (Software/Tech), but **this tool is 100x cheaper** for local businesses (plumbers, realtors, dentists) found on Google Maps.

### **2. Clay.run**
*   **Pros:** The most powerful enrichment engine. Can find personal emails of business owners.
*   **Cons:** Very expensive ($185/mo minimum). Requires significant time to set up complex "waterfalls."
*   **VS This Tool:** Clay is for high-ticket enterprise sales. **This tool is built for speed and volume** at a fraction of the price.

### **3. Outscraper / Apify**
*   **Pros:** Reliable scrapers, stable APIs.
*   **Cons:** Usually just scrape the "info@" email from the site meta-tags. They don't use AI to intelligently find contacts.
*   **VS This Tool:** This tool uses **LLMs (GPT-4o/Llama)** to "read" the website like a human, finding hidden emails and social profiles that basic scrapers miss.

### **4. ZoomInfo**
*   **Pros:** Enterprise-grade B2B database with 300M+ company records.
*   **Cons:** Extremely expensive ($475+/mo). Focused on traditional B2B, not local service businesses.
*   **VS This Tool:** This tool dominates for **local business outreach** (contractors, real estate, services). ZoomInfo can't effectively scrape Google Maps.

### **5. Hunter.io**
*   **Pros:** Great for finding business emails from domain names.
*   **Cons:** Requires you already know the company domains. Doesn't scrape or discover businesses.
*   **VS This Tool:** This tool is **end-to-end**: discover → enrich → export. Hunter is only enrichment.
